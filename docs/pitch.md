# Pitch notes

This file is a placeholder for your Demo Day pitch. In week 11, build your pitch in VentureOS and keep your notes here.

A good pitch is 5 minutes and every slide has proof behind it:

1. The problem, and a real person who has it.
2. Your solution, shown with your live prototype.
3. What users did when they tried it.
4. Your money model.
5. How you will reach customers.
6. What you need next.
